Rhythm

Rhythm is a personal day planner for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. It gives your time a place: work, family, focus and the things you mean to do, planned around the commitments already in your calendars.

Rhythm can read your Apple and Google calendars so nothing gets planned over a real appointment, and it can keep its own "Rhythm" calendar in your Google account so your plan shows up wherever you use Google Calendar.

Rhythm is a personal project in private testing and is not on the App Store.

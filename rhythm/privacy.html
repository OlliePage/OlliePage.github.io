Rhythm privacy policy

Last updated 14 October 2026.

The short version

Rhythm has no servers of its own. Your plan and your calendar data stay on your devices and, if you turn on sync, in your own private iCloud storage. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or sent for analytics.

What Rhythm stores

Your plan, routines, focus history and settings are stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, they are also kept in your private iCloud database so your other devices can see them. Only your Apple Account can read that database.

Apple calendars

With your permission, Rhythm reads events from the calendars you choose on your device so it does not plan over them. It does not change or delete those events.

Google Calendar

If you sign in with Google, Rhythm asks for permission to:

  • see the list of your Google calendars, so you can choose which to plan around;
  • view events on your calendars, so Rhythm does not plan over them;
  • create a secondary calendar called "Rhythm" and manage events on calendars Rhythm created, so your plan appears in Google Calendar. Rhythm cannot change or delete events on any other calendar.

Your Google sign-in is kept in your device's Keychain, on that device only. Google event data is used only to show and plan around your commitments inside Rhythm, and may be copied to your private iCloud database if sync is on. It is never sent anywhere else.

Rhythm's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Signing out of Google in Rhythm removes its Google sign-in from your device. You can also remove Rhythm's access at any time from your Google Account's security settings. Rhythm's Settings can remove your data from the device and from iCloud. Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device.
